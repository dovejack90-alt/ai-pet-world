# Pet Definitions

## MVP roster

| ID | Pet | Rarity | Personality |
|---|---|---|---|
| dog | Dog | Common | Friendly, playful |
| cat | Cat | Common | Curious, independent |
| bunny | Bunny | Common | Shy, playful |
| fox | Fox | Uncommon | Curious, clever |
| panda | Panda | Uncommon | Lazy, friendly |

These are the initial content definitions. Final balance and models come later.

## Personality design

Personality should affect flavour and behaviour while keeping gameplay fair.

Example:

Dog:
- Loves play
- Frequent happy reactions

Cat:
- Curious idle behaviour
- Slightly less predictable activity preference

Bunny:
- Shy reactions
- Strong friendship reward from gentle interactions

Fox:
- Curious reactions
- Exploration preference

Panda:
- Relaxed reactions
- Rest/food preference

No personality should make a starter pet objectively superior.
