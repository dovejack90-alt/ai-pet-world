# Monetisation

## Principles

AI PET WORLD is free to play.

Purchases should be:

- Optional
- Clearly described
- Low friction
- Good value
- Never required to enjoy the core loop

Avoid monetisation that depends on misleading scarcity or paid random rewards.

## Initial price-test range

Candidate price points:

- 5 Robux
- 10 Robux
- 15 Robux
- 25 Robux
- 35 Robux
- 49 Robux
- 99 Robux

These are test points, not promises of final pricing.

## Candidate products

- Small coin pack
- Medium coin pack
- Treat pack
- Toy pack
- Temporary 2x coins boost
- Pet outfit
- House decoration
- Emote
- VIP access

## Economy rules

The free game must provide meaningful progression.

Do not create an artificial wall where spending Robux is necessary to continue.

Track:

- Purchaser conversion
- Revenue per player
- Repeat purchase rate
- Product conversion by item
- Refund/support signals
- Player retention before/after monetisation changes

Any economy change should be tested against player retention, not revenue alone.
