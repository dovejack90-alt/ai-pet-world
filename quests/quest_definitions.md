# Quest Definitions

Initial quest templates:

1. Feed your pet once.
2. Play with your pet once.
3. Visit the park.
4. Visit the cafe.
5. Earn 50 coins.
6. Complete one minigame.
7. Spend coins in the shop.
8. Gain friendship.
9. Visit the forest.
10. Complete three pet interactions.

Quest templates should use IDs, parameters and reward definitions so the quest engine remains generic.
