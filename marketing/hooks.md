# Marketing Hooks

## Pet personality

- I let AI choose my Roblox pet...
- This pet is WAY too dramatic.
- My pet has a personality problem.
- Which personality would you choose?

## Progression

- I started with nothing and raised this pet.
- What happens at maximum friendship?
- I tried to level my pet as fast as possible.

## Collection

- Which pet would you pick?
- I finally unlocked the fox.
- Can you collect every pet?

## Challenge

- Can I finish this challenge before my pet gets hungry?
- I gave myself one hour to build the best pet house.

Hooks should be tested against real performance. Do not assume a hook is good because it sounds good.
