# Marketing

## Positioning

AI PET WORLD should sell the fantasy of having a pet with personality, not the technology behind it.

Core promise:

**Your pet isn't just a pet. It has a personality.**

## Content pillars

1. Funny pet behaviour
2. Pet progression
3. Rare/cool cosmetics
4. Player challenges
5. Behind-the-scenes AI/game development
6. Updates and events

## Example hooks

- I let AI choose my Roblox pet...
- This pet has the WORST personality.
- My Roblox pet did WHAT?
- What happens when your pet reaches level 100?
- I raised the rarest pet in Roblox.
- This pet has a secret ability...
- Which pet would you choose?
- I gave my pet the weirdest outfit possible.

Never make a claim about rarity, scarcity, abilities or rewards unless it is actually true in the game.

## Production pipeline

vidIQ:
- Find relevant demand/trends
- Study competing Roblox content
- Generate/validate hooks

ChatGPT:
- Turn findings into concepts
- Write scripts
- Adapt concepts to the current game build

Canva:
- Thumbnails
- Social graphics
- UI/promotional graphics
- Brand assets

Runway:
- Trailer footage
- Short-form video assets
- Promotional edits

Metricool:
- Schedule approved content
- Review social performance

## Launch cadence

Start content during development.

Initial target:

- 3–5 short-form videos per week
- 1 stronger trailer/update asset per meaningful release
- Reuse winning concepts with new game footage

Do not spam identical content. Double down on concepts that produce actual Roblox visits and healthy player behaviour.
